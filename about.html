ABOUT

About UMBRASEC.

UMBRASEC is an independent defensive security practice working with clients anywhere. It publishes detection engineering, threat analysis, and open-source defensive tools in the open - work you can read, check against its sources, and run in your own environment - and takes on scoped advisory work for businesses that want the same method run with them.

What it publishes

Technical, defender-focused writeups: detection-engineering guides with rules you can run, breakdowns of real techniques mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, mitigation guidance, and honest analysis of current threats and CVEs. Every factual claim is tied to a primary source - vendor advisories, ATT&CK, CVE records, or reputable reporting.

What it deliberately won't publish

No working exploits, no malware, no jailbreak libraries, no offensive tooling. The research describes how attacks work only to the depth a defender needs to detect and stop them. This is a deliberate scope choice: shipping functional attack material would make the project a liability, not an asset. I study how attacks are actually run so defensive advice is not cargo-cult, but offensive notes live separately and are not part of this practice.

On metrics

You won't see report counts, disclosure tallies, or client logos here - padding a record with numbers nobody can verify is the opposite of the point. A site advertising figures it can't substantiate is a red flag, and rightly so. The approach here is the boring, honest one: publish genuinely useful work and let it be checked.

Who's behind it

UMBRASEC is run by 0xdev1, an independent security practitioner based in Australia. I have spent seven years in governance, risk and compliance and in detection engineering, including regulated and public-sector environments. That work does not belong on a billboard, which is why this site uses a handle rather than a legal name. The independent practice is new. The underlying trade is not.

What matters for trust isn't the name on it - it's whether the rules run, the sources check out, and the analysis holds up. All three are things you can verify yourself.

On AI

I use AI tools to draft, maintain, and convert material. Technical claims stay tied to primary sources. Detections, recommendations, and anything that would go to a client or a board are reviewed and owned by me.

Working together

Alongside the published work, UMBRASEC takes on scoped defensive engagements - Microsoft 365 and Entra ID reviews, detection engineering, and virtual-CISO advisory. The services page sets out the method and the shape of each engagement. Scoping conversations are no-obligation, you meet me on a call, and the work is scoped in writing before any money moves. Advisory is defensive: no red teaming, and not a 24/7 incident-response retainer.

Corrections welcome

If a detection misbehaves, a citation is wrong, or an analysis is off, that's exactly the feedback this project wants. Open an issue on GitHub or email 0xdev1@umbrasec.dev. Corrections make the next version better and are credited.

Standing commitments: defensive scope only · every claim sourced · free to read, no paywall or email gate · honest about what exists and what doesn't.
